Living a non-commit life

Don't commit to an industry. Don't commit to a friend group. Don't commit to anyone. Only commit to yourself.

Let everything come as it comes. Do it if you want to. Don't if you don't. Don't build your self identity on external things. Live as you feel.

Live without expectation. Just do things. Feel life. Don't think about everything that already happened, hasn't happened, or might happen.

Don't let other people's ego cross your boundaries. Don't become a sacrifice in someone else's story. Live for yourself, and only for yourself.
