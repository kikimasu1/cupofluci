Yesterday I wanted to order some contacts because I'm running out of the ones I brought from the states. I have astigmatism, so I'm very particular about the brand.

However, I looked through all the major contact lens platforms, including the official website of J&J, only to find out that Japanese Acuvue Oasys manufacturers don't make my axis (100).

I think it's hilarious, because if I hadn't gotten my eyes examined in the states, I wouldn't have known the real measurements for my right eye. I feel like my true colors reveal themselves in certain places. My life goal is to find these places & the ppl I feel comfortable around.

I will be myself and stick to my values, because that represents me. I don't care what others do or think. This is my life, I'm living it the way I want.

By being authentic to myself, I can attract what's the best for me, and I can live/create with love, not fear.
