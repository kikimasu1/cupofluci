Healthcare is really expensive in the states. When I was a student, dental & vision insurance wasn't included in the student health plan. And I was not very aware of their importance, thus I brought olive oil just to stop my teeth from aching. (which wasn't very helpful)



I got my teeth examined & got 3 root canals & crowns after getting a full-time job. Thanks to the high yearly reimbursement rates, I get to go to dentists without fearing that it might break my bank.



One of the reasons why I left the US was how expensive things were in the healthcare system. I feel like I have to have a full-time job to support everything.



As long as people got income/ tax/ healthcare figured out, they can live anywhere on this planet, right?
