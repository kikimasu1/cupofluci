To me, respect comes first, whatever the relationship is.

Whether it's family bonds, friendship, or romantic relationships, if there's no respect, you aren't treating others as individuals with the same recognition power as you. It's hard to cultivate real closeness.

It's just you and your projection.

PS: To know more about how to treat others as individuals, I highly recommend **[I and Thou](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F046dz0&q=me+and+thou+i+and+thou)** by **[Martin Buber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Buber)**.
