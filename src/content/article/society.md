It's hard for one to live without society. Living means interacting with different kinds of people. Studying, working, building a startup, you will be dealing with people either way.

**TL;DR:** How do you interact well with society? To me, it's finding (not necessarily maximizing) the intersection between creating value for society and realizing self-worth.

Back to the first principles of living. **Life itself is the purpose.** Living is nothing more than simple living. It's not for realizing other goals.

Life is finite & irreversible. So far nobody lives to 150 years old, and nobody grows to 3 meters tall. As an adult, your life is completely in your own hands. Make choices that can increase future possibilities.

How does an adult support themselves? A job / a startup / living off your parents.

(I count living off a partner as a kind of job too. If you want to receive, you have to give. The degree is different, and so is the aspect.)

Startups, to me, are about helping people solve problems. They, or society, pay you accordingly. The best case is when the work is ethical and you actually like it, the return is high, experience keeps adding up, and what you do compounds. Along the way you also meet interesting people.

First, make sure the problem really is one that still needs solving. Then look at what you can offer that is hard to replace.

What's the moat? How did companies doing similar things end up? Where, and when, did they get those results? Why did they succeed or fail? Take it apart step by step. Look through the surface and see what it actually is. Then build an MVP as fast as you can, put it into the real market, get feedback, and repeat.

(Learning is a lot like this. To me, the most important part is act fast + a fast feedback loop.)

A job is best when you can rely on a platform to amplify your strengths, gain experience, and meet different people. While you help someone else get things done, you also pick up transferable skills.

Going back and forth over who's higher is childish. Better to work together and see if you can actually make things happen. If someone meets one of your specific needs, that's enough. Anything else that doesn't hurt your interests, simply don't judge. Nobody likes being looked down on. A bit more tolerance, a bit more love. Everyone is equal. Society can tolerate different ways of living. You only go far when your intention is right.

I like interesting individuals who think outside the box. While others are fighting a rat race, they can see the competition means nothing. There isn't even a track. Life is just life itself. From there, you start to really live life.
