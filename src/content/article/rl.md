When I got back home today, I noticed that an accident involving a person (人身事故 in Japanese) had happened on the line I often take. I was deeply shocked because it was so close.

Sadly, it's very common in Tokyo. People jump onto the tracks for different reasons. Some of them even post their last words online before doing it. They, as citizens of a developed country, have what many people fight their whole lives for, yet they take their own lives.

Also, Japan sits on an earthquake belt, and earthquakes are another common thing here. When I first experienced a big one on 8/23/2026, I thought that might be it. Fortunately, I made it.

There was another time when Japan released a false alarm that a magnitude 9.9 earthquake would hit. I was pretty calm and was about to accept my fate.

These experiences made me reflect on life more often. I'm not sure what will happen in the future, but I'm sure that everybody will be dead at some point. So I'm constantly questioning myself: Is this the LIFE that I want? If not, what can I do to change it? Am I surrounding myself with people I truly admire? If not, how can I make sure I have positive influences around me?

Thinking about death made me wanna make the most of my life more than ever. I appreciate every day 🌞
